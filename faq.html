FAQs · NEMI LMM

Get to know NEMI LMM.

Straight answers to the questions we hear most.

What is the Large Manufacturing Model (LMM)?

One AI system that runs the entire arc of a physical product, from idea and engineering through manufacturing to field feedback. It is delivered through three suites: Anvil (engineering), Orion (the factory OS) and Atlas (field telemetry).

Do you actually manufacture, or just provide software?

We manufacture and apply LMM to provide superiority on what matters: cost, quality, speed, and solving difficult manufacturing challenges.

What can you make?

Tooling and fixtures, special-purpose machines and robotics, precision metal parts to 10–15 μm, battery packs, motors, electronics, plastics and composites, and complete complex assemblies, under one roof.

How fast will I get a quote?

Send an RFQ and we revert with a quote within 24 to 48 hours. For machined, flight-critical parts, our Speedshop has delivered first supply in under a week on a recent programme.

Are you certified for aerospace and regulated work?

Yes. Our factories are certified to AS9100D:2016 (aerospace quality management) and ISO 9001:2015, and hold flight-critical tolerances in serial supply.

Where is NEMI based?

US-headquartered in Stafford, Texas, with owned factories in Chennai and Coimbatore, India, .
