NEMI AI × BNC Motors · Electric motorcycle program

A production-ready EV motorcycle in 9 months.

The LMM's orchestration layer compressed a benchmarked 18-month EV motorcycle development cycle to 9 months, a 50% reduction, taking the BNC Messenger C110 from blank sheet to production-ready.

The Client

BNC is an EV company, a subsidiary of NEMI, building affordable electric vehicles for commercial and last-mile fleets across India, Africa and South Asia. The Messenger C110 is its 100–125 cc-equivalent electric motorcycle for African commercial riders.

The Brief

An electric motorcycle for the African fleet rider.

A 100–125 cc-equivalent electric motorcycle for African commercial riders, boda-boda operators and last-mile fleets.

What we did

Concept to production-ready, in four stages.

The Messenger C110 moved from concept to production-ready through four key stages, each driven by the LMM.

01 · Render

Sketch to 3D

Lumos converts product sketches and 2D requirements into 3D STL renders.

02 · CAD

CAD & schematics

Anvil's generative CAD converts STL to STP and generates the electronic schematics.

03 · Simulation

FEA, thermal & DFM

Anvil's automated meshing and simulation for FEA and thermal; DFM automated too.

04 · Prototype

Build & validate

LMM build packages drive machining, fabrication, 3D printing and electronics assembly for rapid test.
