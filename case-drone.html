NEMI AI · Defense UAS program

Engineering & manufacturing a drone powertrain.

The LMM engineered every core powertrain component, battery, motor, ESC and ground control unit, to build-to-spec standards, and stood up in-house production for all of them, delivering an indigenous supply chain for the customer's defense UAS program.

The Client

A Bengaluru-based aerospace & defense engineering firm founded in 1997, the customer is an AS9100D- and CEMILAC-certified systems-engineering company. A long-standing design partner to India's leading defense and space agencies, it brought NEMI in as its build-to-spec design partner for this UAS program.

What we did

Every core component, in-house.

NEMI engineered every core powertrain component to build-to-spec, production-ready standards, with in-house prototyping, test and production for each.

Battery

Battery pack & BMS

Anvil Mechanical & Electronic CAD generate the battery and BMS designs; in-house assembly to rapidly prototype and test.

Motor

Electromagnetics & mechanical

Anvil's motor-design library with skill files for customization and simulation; in-house machining and motor manufacturing.

ESC

Electronic speed controller

MIL-grade (MIL-STD-810 / 461) controller with FOC firmware, throttle mapping and RPM matching, designed in Electronic CAD.

GCU

Ground control unit

Integrated power-delivery and retraction mechanism designed in Anvil; LMM-orchestrated prototyping and test.
