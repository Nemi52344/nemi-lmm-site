NEMI AI · Consumer appliances program

A vending-machine line, live in 5 weeks.

A consumer-appliances brand engaged NEMI's LMM platform to scale its smart filter-coffee machine from in-house builds to quality-controlled volume production, with minimal-to-zero tooling, lower cost and greater localization.

The Client

A consumer-appliances brand that builds smart, self-cleaning coffee vending machines, brewing authentic South Indian filter coffee at up to 100 cups an hour, serving cafés, offices, hospitals and college campuses.

What we did

Re-engineered, then stood up the line.

Component engineering plus host-cell assembly, jigs, fixtures and SOPs, to scale production to 400 units a month, with the refrigerator localized to spec.

Engineering

Component redesign

Redesign for tooling avoidance and DFM across plastic, sheet metal, casting and machining. Anvil drove the rapid redesign and simulation.

Refrigeration

Localized fridge unit

A sealed, food-grade container with a cartridge architecture for quick swaps, redesigned and localized to the spec provided.

Assembly

Host-cell line

Jigs, fixtures and material-movement equipment for inspection and assembly, with system and process documentation to 400 units/month.

Orchestration

LMM-driven build

Anvil generated the jig and fixture designs; the LMM orchestrated the downstream machining and fabrication, start to finish in 5 weeks.
