Investor Relations

Do for hardware what LLMs did for software.

NEMI is building the Large Manufacturing Model (LMM): one AI system that runs the arc of a physical product, from idea through engineering and production to what is learned in the field.

We train it where the data lives, in our own full-stack factories in India, and sell that capability to customers as Manufacturing-as-a-Service across aerospace, defense, automotive and industrial programs.
